Submit a Claim for Clinical Fact-Checking

Have you seen a suspicious home remedy, forwarded text message, or viral health video? Send the claim to Myth Rx for evidence-based evaluation.

⚠️ Emergency Medical Notice: Myth Rx is an educational health research platform, not an emergency medical service or medical clinic. If you or someone you know is experiencing acute pain, suspected stroke or heart attack, respiratory failure, poisoning, or any medical emergency, please call your local emergency services (e.g. 911 / 112) or visit the nearest hospital emergency room immediately. Do NOT submit a claim or wait for a fact-check.
🔒 Privacy & Medical Records Notice: Please do NOT submit personal medical records, confidential lab results, private identifying health information, or personal diagnoses. Myth Rx evaluates viral public claims and misleading health rumors, not individual patient medical charts.

How We Prioritize Submissions

Due to the immense volume of health misinformation online, our editorial evaluations are prioritized based on:

  • Acute Risk of Harm: Advice urging patients to abandon medications, drink toxic chemicals, or avoid emergency care.
  • Virality: Claims circulating in multiple messaging groups, forums, or high-view video posts.
  • Targeting Vulnerable Patients: Claims promising unverified "cures" for chronic or terminal conditions.
